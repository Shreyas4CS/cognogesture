How it works

Four moves, the same every time. The more pictures you give each gesture, the better it gets.

Add your gestures

Create one for each move: Forward, Reverse, Left, Right, Stop. Name them anything.

Show & record

Hold your sign in front of the camera and press Record. Move it around for 50–100 pictures.

Pick move & speed

Choose what each gesture does and how fast the motors spin (PWM speed).

Drive the maze

Connect the robot, switch to Live and steer with your hand. Unknown signs stop it.
